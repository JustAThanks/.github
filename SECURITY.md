# Security policy

Just a Thanks moves money, so security reports get priority over everything else.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email security@justathanks.com.
Include what you found, how to reproduce it, and what it would let someone do.
Do not open a public issue for a vulnerability.

We confirm receipt within two working days, give you a first assessment within seven, and keep you informed until it is fixed.
We ask for 90 days before public disclosure, or less once a fix has shipped.

## Scope

- justathanks.com and its API.
- The repositories in this organisation.

Out of scope: denial of service, reports from automated scanners with no proof of impact, and anything that needs physical access to a device.

## Safe harbour

Research done in good faith, within this policy, without touching other people's data or money, will not be reported to the authorities by us.
Use test accounts and the smallest payment amounts possible.
