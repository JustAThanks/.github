# Code of conduct

We build a product about gratitude, and we hold ourselves to the same standard.

## Expected

- Be kind and direct.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming anything else.
- Review the work, never the person.
- Keep private information private: payment data, personal data, creator earnings.
- Credit people for their work.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, discrimination or personal attacks of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or reviews.

## Reporting

Write to hello@justathanks.com.
Reports are read by the maintainers, kept confidential, and answered within five working days.
Anyone who breaks this code may be removed from the organisation's repositories and discussions.
